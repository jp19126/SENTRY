## Prospective question and boundary

This study tests richer BERT-to-CNN transfer, transfer coverage, contextual receptive field, and the numerical/hardware cost of a complete CNN guard. It develops evidence for a possible contribution. Standard KD, a conventional dilated residual CNN, and standard integer export alone are not a demonstrated novel FPGA-aware method. The earlier negative CNN adoption decision remains unchanged.

The decision stage is bounded co-design research, not deployment qualification. Nominal 1% benign-calibration FPR is the principal reporting point; 0.5%, 2%, and 5% are secondary. All use a strict margin-versus-threshold decision and complete-document maximum over L256 windows, 254 content positions, 64-token overlap, stride 190, including tails. These reporting points are not safety guarantees or hard admission bounds. Selection is development-only; final checkpoints, arithmetic, and threshold procedure will be frozen before new confirmation scores.

The finite matrix in `configs/kd_cnn_v2.json` has supervised/output-KD controls, original/expanded transfer at matched exposure, richer pooled representation KD, and an equal-capacity receptive-field control. Final principal comparisons use seeds 42, 1337, and 2026. Resource ceilings are 28 fits, 8 GPU hours, 12 vendor-tool hours and 11 vendor dispatches (originally 10; one explicit diagnosed-memory-repair amendment is recorded below), with 40% initially reserved for confirmation/integration; estimates are approximately 2.5 GPU and 6 vendor hours, to be corrected from actual runs. These are engineering limits, not scientific thresholds. Weekly account usage starts at 12% used and must pause at 90% used; no reset is assumed or requested.
